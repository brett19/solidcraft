#### WR-786. `SmCrvOnSurf::EvaluatePoint` and `SmCrvOnSurf::Evaluate` give different points when the UV curve leaves a B-spline surface's domain
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Curves
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmCrvOnSurf.cpp:619` (`SmCrvOnSurf::EvaluatePoint`, def. `:607`) calls the surface's `EvaluatePoint`; `:514` and `:529` (`SmCrvOnSurf::Evaluate`, def. `:449`) call the surface's `Evaluate`. For an `SmBSplineSurface`, `Evaluate` clamps the (u, v) to the natural domain when out-of-bounds evaluation is off (`source/SMLib/src/SmBSplineSurfaceNLib.cpp:8215`, in `SmBSplineSurface::Evaluate`, def. `:8131`), and `EvaluatePoint` does not clamp (its clamp is compiled out by `#if 0  // (don't clamp)` at `:8715`, with a note that clamping made regressions worse).
- **What happens:** one curve on surface gives two positions at one parameter. When the UV curve's point at `t` lies outside the surface's natural domain and the surface's out-of-bounds flag is off (the default), `EvaluatePoint(t)` returns the surface continued past its domain, while `Evaluate(t, n)` returns the surface at the clamped (u, v), with the derivatives lifted from the partials at that clamped point. The distance between the two is the overshoot times the surface's partials, to first order. `SmCrvOnSurf.cpp:513` says the clamp is meant ("clamps UVCurvePt to m_pSurface domain when IsOutOfBoundsEnabled() == FALSE"); the defect is that the curve's two evaluators do not follow one rule, so a caller that takes the point from one and the tangent from the other works with a point and a tangent from different places. Upstream meets such UV curves in practice: the debug check that would report them (`:496`-`:511`) asserts only when `SM_NMTLIB_7166` is defined, and its comment says fillet rail curves built badly trigger it (Fillet Case 231) and that the messages are skipped until the tolerance-model changes.
- **Trigger:** the bilinear B-spline plane S(u, v) = (u, v, 0) on [0, 1] x [0, 1] (degree 1 x 1, knots 0 0 1 1 each way), and a curve on it whose UV curve is the line from (0.5, 0.5) to (1.5, 0.5). At the line's end: `EvaluatePoint` gives (1.5, 0.5, 0), the closed form; `Evaluate` gives the point (1, 0.5, 0) and the tangent (1, 0, 0). The two points are 0.5 apart.
- **How found:** auditing the callers of the clamped and continued evaluators for tangency-labs/solidcraft#15759.
- **Confirmed:** by reading the C++ (the lines above) and on the port, which follows these lines one for one (`solidcore/crates/solidcore/src/curve/crv_on_surf.rs:882` and `:966` at SolidCraft 2ff59f79): a probe of the trigger above measured exactly the values given there. Not run against the C++ build. No downstream wrong result (a wrong body or a failed operation) has been traced to it yet.
- **Rust port:** affected in the same way (the port is faithful here).
